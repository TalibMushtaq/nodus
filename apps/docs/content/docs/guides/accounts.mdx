---
title: Accounts and sessions
description: Register, sign in, recover, change your password, and end sessions.
---

Nodus accounts are created against a Relay. Authentication uses Argon2id
password hashing and opaque, server-side sessions delivered as an HttpOnly
cookie (web) or stored in the OS keychain (mobile). There are no JWTs and no
tokens exposed to client-side JavaScript.

## Create an account

1. Open `/auth` in the web client (or the sign-in screen in the mobile app).
2. Choose **Create account**, pick an email and password.
3. The client derives a **24-word recovery phrase**, shows it once, and requires
   you to confirm you have saved it.
4. Only the recovery public key is enrolled — the phrase itself is never stored
   server-side.

<Callout type="warn">
  Store the recovery phrase somewhere safe and offline. It is the only way to
  recover the account if all trusted devices are lost.
</Callout>

## Sign in

Enter your email and password. On success the Relay issues a session; the web
client proxies it into a hardened cookie (HttpOnly, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` in
production).

## Recover an account

If you can reach the Relay:

1. Choose **Recover** on the auth screen.
2. Request a recovery challenge and sign it with the recovery key.
3. The Relay verifies the signature and issues a new session.

If the Internet is unavailable but a paired Storage Node is on the LAN, recovery
can be performed locally against the node. See
[Recovery](/docs/guides/recovery).

## Change your password

**Settings → Change password** re-verifies your current password and rotates
the session.

## Sign out

- **Sign out** ends the current session.
- **Sign out all devices** (Settings, or Security) revokes every other session
  while keeping the caller signed in.

A revoked or expired session is rejected by the Relay WebSocket with close code
`4001`, which signs the client out cleanly.
