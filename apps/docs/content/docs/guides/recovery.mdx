title: Recovery
description: Recover an account with your phrase, offline via a node, or rotate the key.
---

If you lose your only trusted device, the recovery phrase is how you get back
in. See [Recovery](/docs/concepts/recovery) for the mechanism;
[ADR-0002](/docs/adr/0002-recovery-mechanism) for the decision.

## Recover with the phrase

**Online (Relay reachable):**

1. On the auth screen choose **Recover**.
2. Enter the 24-word phrase.
3. The client requests a signed challenge, signs it locally, and submits it.
4. The Relay verifies the signature and issues a session.

**Offline (Storage Node on the LAN):**

1. Ensure a paired Storage Node is reachable on the local network.
2. Start recovery as above; the client falls back to the node's
   challenge-response endpoint.
3. On success, envelopes are unlocked locally — no Internet needed.

## Reveal the phrase again

On the **Security** page you can reveal and copy the stored phrase, but only
after re-entering your account password. The phrase is sealed at rest on the
device.

## Rotate the recovery key

If the phrase may have been exposed, regenerate it. Rotation derives a new
recovery key and re-seals key envelopes to it. Do this from **Security** in
either client.
