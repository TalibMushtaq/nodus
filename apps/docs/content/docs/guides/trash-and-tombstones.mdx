---
title: Trash and tombstones
description: Soft-delete, restore, and permanently purge files and folders.
---

Deleting in Nodus is soft by default. A delete writes a **tombstone** so the
action converges across nodes, and the item remains recoverable until it is
purged.

## Restore or purge

Open **Tombstone** in the web client (the **Trash** screen on mobile):

- each row shows the item and, where relevant, per-node status and days until
  automatic purge;
- **Restore** undoes the soft delete;
- **Delete forever** (purge) removes the entity and its objects permanently,
  across the Relay and nodes.

<Callout type="warn">
  Purge is destructive and cannot be undone. Prefer letting the retention window
  elapse if you are unsure.
</Callout>

## Retention

Tombstones are retained for a configurable window before automatic purge. See
[Garbage collection](/docs/concepts/garbage-collection) and
[ADR-0005](/docs/adr/0005-garbage-collection-policy).
